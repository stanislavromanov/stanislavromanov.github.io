Works > Cisco

I developed a marketing website for Cisco using PHP and WordPress. The site was designed to promote their “Reimagining Collaboration” event, featuring information about the event, keynote speakers, and reasons to attend. It included an engaging live stream section, a registration form with prize incentives, and integration with social media for broader reach. By leveraging WordPress, the platform offered flexibility for content updates and seamless user experience. The visually appealing design, responsive layout, and effective call-to-actions helped Cisco connect with their audience and drive event registrations successfully.
