Works > Transport UK

Developed the frontend, CI/CD pipeline, and contributed to the C# backend for a transit operations management system used by London bus controllers. The platform features real-time schedule monitoring, event and ticket management with hierarchical categorization, run-out fleet assignments, and mileage performance dashboards with per-depot and per-route breakdowns. Built with React, TypeScript, and Zustand, integrating Microsoft OAuth and a .NET API. Worked in a team of 3 to deliver a tool that streamlines daily depot operations from driver sign-on to end-of-day reporting.
